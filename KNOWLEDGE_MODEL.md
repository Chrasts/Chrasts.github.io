# Knowledge graph policy

The Knowledge branch is a map of substantive working knowledge, not a transcript, interest list or keyword inventory.

## Inclusion rule

A Knowledge node should be supported by at least one strong signal such as sustained study, independent study substantial enough to use the material, research/thesis work, a real software or data project, or repeated professional practice. A completed course is evidence, but it is neither necessary nor sufficient by itself.

Interests and intended future learning belong under About or Education until they become working knowledge. Small techniques stay inside a broader node unless they have independent explanatory value in the graph.

## Status language

Statuses describe the basis/depth of knowledge rather than pretending to be standardized proficiency scores. Examples used in the graph include `core area`, `strong foundation`, `working foundation`, `research use`, `thesis focus`, `applied`, `independent study`, and `introductory`.

## Current mathematical organization

`Mathematics & Logic` separates Mathematical Logic, Universal Algebra, Lattice Theory, Set Theory, and Number Theory & Arithmetic. Universal Algebra and Lattice Theory are related but not nested: they are distinct fields connected by typed graph edges and by the algebraic-logic work that uses both.

Mathematical Logic is allowed to go deeper where the distinctions are genuinely informative: First-order Logic & Metalogic, Incompleteness & Arithmetization, Model Theory, Proof Theory & Formal Calculi, Modal Logic, Algebraic Logic, and Computational Logic. Thesis-specific depth is represented under Algebraic Logic and Universal Algebra rather than by creating a node for every lemma or construction.

## Maintenance rule

When adding a node, ask:

1. Can the knowledge be evidenced by actual work, sustained study, or repeated practice?
2. Does the concept deserve independent navigation rather than being a detail of its parent?
3. Would the label still be defensible if the associated course title or current project disappeared?
4. Is this knowledge rather than merely an interest, planned study, or a tool used once?

If the answer to these is weak, keep it as metadata, project technology, an About interest, or omit it.
